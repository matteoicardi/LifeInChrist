## The two principal programme resources

These are complementary rather than competing options. Sycamore supplies a natural six-part journey; Decision Point supplies a rich collection of short films, activities and substantial leader material.

<div class="resource-grid">
  <section class="resource-card resource-card-featured">
    <p class="resource-label">British · Catholic · six sessions</p>
    <h3><a href="https://www.sycamore.fm/pathways/c-sacramental-pathways/confirmation-short/">Sycamore Confirmation Short Course</a></h3>
    <p>A conversation-based pathway through happiness, Jesus, prayer, Baptism and Confirmation, forgiveness and mission, and the Eucharist. Core films and guides are free after leader registration; downloads require a subscription.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="resource-card resource-card-featured">
    <p class="resource-label">Free · 12 sessions · 72 short films</p>
    <h3><a href="https://www.dynamiccatholic.com/confirmation.html">Decision Point</a></h3>
    <p>A polished American programme built for teenagers, with short films, a complete student workbook and a detailed leader guide. Its strongest sessions can enrich the 2026 journey without forcing twelve sessions into eight meetings.</p>
  </section>
</div>

## Other Confirmation programmes reviewed

<div class="resource-grid">
  <section class="resource-card">
    <h3><a href="https://www.dioceseofnottingham.uk/resources/transformed-in-christ">Transformed in Christ</a></h3>
    <p>A 22-session Catholic programme for ages 12–16. It is explicitly evangelistic, places the kerygma and prayer at the heart of every session, and includes an encounter retreat. The books are purchased resources and the full course is much longer than our timetable.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="resource-card">
    <h3><a href="https://ascensionpress.com/pages/program/chosen-your-journey-to-confirmation">Chosen: Your Journey to Confirmation</a></h3>
    <p>A substantial 24-session American video course from Ascension, with student and leader materials. Attractive and thoroughly Catholic, but commercial and designed for a much longer programme.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="resource-card">
    <h3><a href="https://www.liverpoolcatholic.org.uk/department/pastoral-development/confirmation-programme">Called / Life with Christ Is a Wonderful Adventure</a></h3>
    <p>A free collection from the Archdiocese of Liverpool with games, prayer, parish activities, the Good News and practical administration material. Liverpool presents it as parish enrichment alongside catechesis supplied elsewhere, so it works best as an activity toolbox.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="resource-card">
    <h3><a href="https://dioceseofsalford.org.uk/empowered-confirmation-programme-sees-successful-zoom-launch/">EMPOWERED</a></h3>
    <p>A free and editable Salford programme organised around the seven gifts of the Holy Spirit, with slides, Scripture, saints, games, prayer and material on the Rite of Confirmation. Useful selectively, especially for the gifts and the rite.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="resource-card">
    <h3><a href="https://alpha.org.uk/youth">Alpha Youth</a></h3>
    <p>A free, highly accessible introduction to life, faith, Jesus, prayer and the Holy Spirit. Excellent for evangelisation and honest discussion, but it needs specifically Catholic teaching on Confirmation, Reconciliation and the Eucharist alongside it.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="resource-card">
    <h3><a href="https://www.pflaum.com/confirmation-program/">Gifted with the Spirit</a></h3>
    <p>An eight-lesson Catholic programme for junior- and senior-high ages. Each lesson moves through experience, teaching and response, with Scripture, prayer and activities. It is a conventional purchased catechetical course.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="resource-card">
    <h3><a href="http://sdc.me.uk/confirmation/default.html">SDC Confirmation Resources</a></h3>
    <p>A free and complete 12-session course, plus two supplementary units, from the Society of the Doctrine of Christ. It offers plans, worksheets, prayer sheets, key words and PowerPoints. Thorough but strongly classroom-led; most useful as a source of individual activities.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="resource-card">
    <h3><a href="https://www.intergenerationalfaith.com/sacraments--rituals.html">Intergenerational Confirmation Programme</a></h3>
    <p>Free PDF, Word and home-activity resources designed to involve different ages and families in learning about the words, symbols and rite of Confirmation. Useful for parent or parish sessions rather than as the main teenage pathway.</p>
  </section>
</div>

## Prayer and Scripture

Preparation is not only learning about God; it is learning to spend time with him.

- [The Rosary guide from NDCYS](https://www.ndcys.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Rosary.pdf).
- [Prayers for teenagers and parents](https://holycomforterparish.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/01/Teen-and-Parent-Prayer-Booklet.pdf).
- [Veni Creator Spiritus](https://www.cbcew.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2025/05/GoS-Conclave.pdf) – the Church's ancient hymn asking the Holy Spirit to come; especially suitable before Confirmation.
- [Anima Christi](https://www.vatican.va/archive/compendium_ccc/documents/archive_2005_compendium-ccc_en.html) – a prayer of union with Christ, printed in the prayer appendix to the Catechism's Compendium and especially suitable before or after Holy Communion.
- [The Suscipe of Saint Ignatius](https://www.ignatianspirituality.com/ignatian-prayer/prayers-by-st-ignatius-and-others/suscipe-the-radical-prayer/) – a prayer offering our whole life to God.
- [Introduction to Lectio Divina](https://youtu.be/gKYEOc3ik9k) – a simple way to pray with Scripture.
- [Read Me When: Scripture for different moments](https://www.ndcys.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Read-me-when.pdf).
- [Bible in a Year reading plan](https://bnspcatholic.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/the-official-365-day-reading-plan-for-the-bible-in-a-year.pdf).

Silence is prayer too. Sit before God and begin simply: **“Here I am. What do you want to tell me?”**

## Understanding Confirmation

- [Catechism of the Catholic Church 1285–1321](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_two/section_two/chapter_one/article_2.html) – the Church's teaching on Confirmation.
- [The effects of Confirmation: Catechism 1302–1310](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_two/section_two/chapter_one/article_2/iii_the_effects_of_confirmation.html).
- [What happens at Confirmation?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=49tLYYagp2Q).
- [Bishop Barron on Confirmation, the gifts and evangelisation](https://youtu.be/Ynj_nOxC-1Y).
- [Confirmation SketchPad](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lu3MoT_egFI).
- [3 Minute Catechism](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLIcePO_eJb2_EElTdFm1PFLNkH17EQcV-) – short animated explanations of the foundations of Catholic faith.

Confirmation is not graduation from the Church. Christ strengthens the baptised through a special outpouring of the Holy Spirit, unites them more firmly to himself and sends them to witness to the faith.

## Saints and Confirmation names

Candidates may choose a saint whose life can inspire and accompany them. Good starting points include Saint Maria Goretti, Saint Pier Giorgio Frassati, Saint Carlo Acutis, Saint Thérèse of Lisieux, Saint Dominic Savio, Saint Kateri Tekakwitha, Saint Josephine Bakhita, Saint Maximilian Kolbe, Saint Teresa Benedicta of the Cross and the English Martyrs.

Pier Giorgio Frassati and Carlo Acutis were canonised on 7 September 2025 and are now both saints.

- [Saints A–Z](https://mycatholic.life/saints/)
- [Vatican News: Saint of the Day](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/saints.html)
- [EWTN saints and holy figures](https://www.ewtn.com/catholicism/saints)
