## Monastic Retreats (Abbeys & Priories)

These centres offer a unique opportunity to share in the prayer life of a religious community. Most offer "Monastic Hospitality" where guests can join the community for Mass and the Divine Office.

| Centre | Location | Structure | Accommodation | Link |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ampleforth Abbey** | North Yorks | Benedictine Abbey | High-quality en-suite and standard rooms; Alban Roe House for large groups. | [ampleforthabbey.org.uk](https://www.ampleforthabbey.org.uk/retreats) |
| **Douai Abbey** | Berkshire | Benedictine Abbey | 22 modern en-suite rooms. *Note: Self-catering cottages are expected to close in 2025.* | [douaiabbey.org.uk](https://www.douaiabbey.org.uk/guesthouse.php) |
| **Buckfast Abbey** | Devon | Benedictine Abbey | Monastery (Men), St Petroc's (Women/Couples), and a hotel on-site. | [buckfast.org.uk](https://www.buckfast.org.uk/retreats) |
| **Mount St Bernard** | Leicestershire | Cistercian Abbey | Silent, traditional monastic guesthouse. Ideal for deep quiet. | [mountstbernard.org](https://www.mountstbernard.org/guesthouse) |
| **Worth Abbey** | West Sussex | Benedictine Abbey | St Bruno's Retreat Centre (31 guests, mostly en-suite). | [worthabbey.net](https://worthabbey.net/retreats/retreat-programme/) |
| **Belmont Abbey** | Hereford | Benedictine Abbey | Hedley Lodge (17 en-suite rooms) and quieter monastic options. | [belmontabbey.org.uk](https://www.belmontabbey.org.uk/hospitality) |
| **Quarr Abbey** | Isle of Wight | Benedictine Abbey | Simple rooms (some en-suite for women) and self-catering flats. | [quarrabbey.org](https://www.quarrabbey.org/visit-us/guesthouse) |
| **Prinknash Abbey** | Gloucestershire | Benedictine Abbey | Limited monastic rooms (Men only); day retreats and cafe. | [prinknashabbey.org](https://www.prinknashabbey.org/) |
| **Boars Hill Priory** | Oxford | Carmelite (OCD) | 27 rooms (many en-suite) in 17 acres of woodland. | [carmelite.org.uk](https://www.carmelite.org.uk/retreats/boars-hill/) |
| **Aylesford Priory** | Kent | Carmelite (OC) | Large historic guesthouse; "The Friars" is a major pilgrimage site. | [thefriars.org.uk](https://www.thefriars.org.uk/) |

## Spiritual Centres & Houses of Prayer

Focusing on spiritual direction, specific retreat themes (like Ignatian spirituality), or community living.

| Centre | Location | Structure | Accommodation | Link |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **St Beuno's** | North Wales | Jesuit Centre | The UK's premier centre for Ignatian retreats and silence. | [beunos.com](https://www.beunos.com/) |
| **Rosmini Centre** | Leicestershire | House of Prayer | Gentili House: 8 en-suite single rooms with communal kitchen. | [rosminians.org.uk](https://www.rosminians.org.uk/the-rosmini-centre/) |
| **Launde Abbey** | Leicestershire | Ecumenical | Stunning 12th-century priory; 39 en-suite bedrooms. | [laundeabbey.org.uk](https://www.laundeabbey.org.uk/) |
| **Madonna House** | North Yorks | Lay Community | "Hospitality of the heart" in Robin Hood's Bay; features a *poustinia*. | [madonnahouse.org](https://www.madonnahouse.org/fieldhouse/robin-hoods-bay/) |
| **House of the Open Door** | Worcestershire | Lay Community | Self-catering retreat house and cottage for individuals and families. | [houseoftheopendoor.org](https://www.houseoftheopendoor.org/) |
| **Dupuis Centre** | Birmingham | Sisters of Charity | 10 en-suite rooms in a peaceful walled garden (Selly Park). | [sellypark.org](https://www.sellypark.org/dupuis-centre/) |
| **Centre for Unity** | Herts | Focolare | Conference and retreat centre in Welwyn Garden City. | [focolare.org.uk](https://www.focolare.org.uk/centre-for-unity-welwyn-garden-city/) |
