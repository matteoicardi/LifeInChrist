## Organisations (general links)

### Youth (18-35)
- [Youth 2000](http://www.youth2000.org/) - Catholic youth retreats and prayer festivals (some events open to families)
- [Jesus Youth](https://www.jesusyouth.co.uk) - Catholic missionary movement with retreats, prayer festivals and ministries
- [Flame](https://cymfed.org.uk/flame2027/) - Catholic youth event (next national gathering at Wembley on Saturday, 6 March 2027, open to **Year 9 up to university age/young adults**)
- [Joel's Bar](https://www.joelsbar.com) - Catholic residential retreat for young adults (18-28), from the Celebrate movement

### Teens
- [The Ascent](http://www.theascentuk.co.uk/) - Catholic three-year discipleship programme (15-17)
- [Fanning the Flame](https://www.fanningtheflame.co.uk) - Catholic summer camp for 12+
- [Faith Movement](https://www.faith.org.uk/) - Catholic movement with events and youth programmes (including summer camps/sessions)
- [Jesus Youth Teens](https://www.jesusyouth.co.uk/teens-ministry.html) - Jesus Youth teens ministry
- [Sion Youth](https://sioncommunity.org.uk/) - Youth ministry of the Sion Community, offering D-Weekends, youth retreats, and schools ministry
- [Generation Hope - Mary's Meals](https://www.gen-hope.org/) - Glasgow-based movement with retreats for Scottish Catholic secondary schools (12-15)

### Families
- [Celebrate (Trust)](https://www.celebratetrust.org/) - Catholic charismatic family events (including the Celebrate Festival)
- [Craig Lodge](https://www.craiglodge.org/) - Catholic family house of prayer in the Scottish Highlands (family weeks, retreats and Stronghold worship festival)
- [Sion Community](https://sioncommunity.org.uk/) - Catholic community with retreats and festivals (often family-focused)
- [We Believe](https://www.webelievefestival.com) - Catholic festival aimed at families
- [New Dawn](https://princeofpeace.org.uk/new-dawn/) - Catholic charismatic family conferences and retreats (Manchester/Walsingham). *Note: The August 2026 Walsingham conference was cancelled due to a community sabbatical.*
- [Chemin Neuf - Cana](https://www.chemin-neuf.org.uk/programmes/cana/) - Catholic community (*with ecumenical vocation*), supporting couples and families (Cana weeks/retreats)

### Diocesan youth services
- [Kenelm Youth Trust](https://www.kenelmyouthtrust.org.uk) - Summer camps (11-18), pilgrimages, retreats and youth events (Archdiocese of Birmingham, open to all)
- [Notts Diocesan Youth Service](https://www.ndcys.com) - Events, retreats and resources; The Briars retreat centre hosts open events

### Marriage
- [Teams of Our Lady](https://www.teamsgb.org.uk/) - Catholic movement for married couples
- [Theology of the Body UK Network](https://www.tobnetworkuk.com) - Online group promoting Theology of the Body in the UK

### Europe (Catholic)
- [Notre-Dame de Chrétienté (Paris-Chartres)](https://www.nd-chretiente.com) - Pentecost pilgrimage (France)
- [Emmanuel Community (Paray-le-Monial)](https://emmanuel.info) - Catholic community running summer sessions and retreats (France)
- [Jesus Festival](https://jesusfestival.fr/) - Catholic music festival (France)
